[OBJECT:ENTITY]

The Lua-port companion to the static entity patch. Every reaction generated by
scripts/creationforge.lua carries [FORTRESS_MODE_ENABLED], so NO [PERMITTED_REACTION] is needed —
that is why this file is ~10 lines instead of the 22,510-line entity_patch_creationforge.txt.

Only the two custom workshops still need an entity permit (buildings are not Lua-generatable).
To support another civ/mod, copy the block and change the [SELECT_ENTITY] id.

[SELECT_ENTITY:MOUNTAIN]
	[PERMITTED_BUILDING:CREATION_FORGE]
	[PERMITTED_BUILDING:TRAINING_FORGE]
